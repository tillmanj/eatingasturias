In Spain, a *comarca* is a traditional territorial division, often informal, that comprises multiple other legal entities such as counties or municipalities. Usually a comarca is seen as having some sort of defining trait that ties it together; be it economic, geographic, cultural, or linguistic.

What this means in a legalistic sense is that the regional governments of the autonomous communities of Spain have created their own territorial divisions that do not map exactly onto the boundaries of the Provinces, which are defined by the central government. Asturias has eight of these *comarcas*, defined by the regional government, but without much in the way of devolved powers. In addition, there are a small number of what can only be described as marketing *comarcas*; such as the *comarca de la sidra* that lack even popular recognition, and exist for the promotion of products or tourist experiences.

For the purposes of Asturian territorial division, a *comarca* is equivalent to the English County (with it's subdivision the district) and the *[conceyo](/visit/conceyos/)* the equivalent to the Englsih district or the American county, with no subdivision.

## Eo-Navia
The comarca of Eo-Navia contains 17 conceyos: Valdés, Navia, Villayón, Cuaña, Boal, Eilao, Pezós, Grandas de Salime, El Franco, Tapia, Samartín d'Ozcos, Santalla d'Ozcos, Vilanova d'Ozcos, Castropol, Veiga d'Eo, Santiso d'Abres, Taramundi
## Narcea
The comarca of Narcea contains 5 conceyos: Ibias, Degaña, Cangas del Narcea, Allande, Tineo
## Avilés
The comarca of Avilés contains 10 conceyos: Avilés, Candamo, Castrillón, Corvera de Asturias, Cudillero, Gozón, Illas, Muros de Nalón, Pravia, Soto del Barco
## Oviedo
The comarca of Oviedo contains 21 conceyos: Cabranes, Nava, Sariegu, Bimenes, Siero, Noreña, Oviedo, Llanera, Les Regueres, Ribera de Arriba, Morcín, Riosa, Santu Adrianu, Grau, Quirós, Teberga, Proaza, Yernes y Tameza, Somiedu, Salas, Miranda
## Gijón
The comarca of Gijón contains 3 conceyos: Villaviciosa, Gijón, Carreño
## Nalón
The comarca of Nalón contains 5 conceyos: Caso, Sobrescobio, Laviana, San Martín del Rey Aurelio, Langreo
## Caudal
the comarca of Caudal contains 3 conceyos: Lena, Aller, Mieres
## Oriente
The comarca of Oriente contains 14 conceyos: Ribadedeva, Peñamellera Baxa, Peñamellera Alta, Cabrales, Llanes, Onís, Cangues d'Onís, Ribesella, Amieva, Parres, Caravia, Colunga, Piloña, Ponga
