Perhaps the best place to start talking about Asturian cuisine is with a deceptively simple question: what is Asturian cuisine all about?

# The Short Answer
When Asturians talk about their own food (a good place to start, eh?) they usually tend towards phrases like _platos de cuchara_ and _a fuego lento_. Spain's official tourist website in English describes Asturian food as "slow cooking over low heat".

## The Four Pillars
Asturian cuisine is a broad table, held up by four sturdy legs. Those legs are the apple, the bean, seafood, and cheese. Take any one of them away, and the idea of Asturian food as a distinct entity ceases to exist.
