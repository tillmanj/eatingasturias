{% newthought 'The conceyo of Gijón' %} contains just over a quarter of the total population of Asturias. The capital of the coneyo, also named Gijón, as is common, is therefore the largest city in Asturias, with a population of approximately 250,000.

It has long been the beating heart of Asturias. While Oviedo is the capital, and the cultural center, Gijón rose to prominence as the commercial and industrial center of Asturias. These days it is the premier travel destination in Asturias.
