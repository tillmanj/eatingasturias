The Christmas market in Oviedo is (by number of vendors) the largest in Spain. For 2026 it runs from the 27<sup>th</sup> of November to the 6<sup>th</sup> of January. Ranging over the plazas in front of the Cathedral de Oviedo and the surrounding streets, the make up of the market vendors in 2026 is:

- 46 artisans selling individual production
- 44 stalls selling Christmas decorations and small gifts
- 16 food stalls, focusing on Christmas treats

The market officially opens on the 27<sup>th</sup> of November with the illumination of the Christmas lights in Oviedo. That also happens to be Black Friday (which has become a big shopping day in Spain despite not having Thanksgiving)

In addition to the market stalls, there will be other features of the market, including:

- **Ice rink on Paseo del Bombé**: natural, covered (skating even if it rains), and at least 500 m², with skate rental, support for beginners, and access times. Maximum price: €9 per person per hour.
- **Ferris wheel in the Rosaleda promenade**: Christmas-themed and over twelve meters high, next to inflatables and attractions for children aged 4 to 12. Ride for a maximum of €9.
- **Winter Garden on Paseo de la Herradura**: a food court of up to 500 m² with stalls, bars, and a terrace, offering Christmas products and Asturian cuisine. Open until 11:00 PM from Sunday to Thursday and until midnight on Fridays, Saturdays, and the eves of public holidays.
