{% newthought 'Zea mays, the humble corn plant'%} was cultivated for thousands of years before it made its way to Spain.

## History
First planted in Asturias (and possibly the first planting in Europe) by Gonzalo Méndes de Cancio in the year 1603 in Tapia de Casariego. 

*For the complete history of corn in Asturias, please see the [History of Maíz in Asturias](/culture/history/early-modern/maíz.html) article.*

## Uses in Asturian Cooking
Due to the longstanding aversion to corn, it does not appear in its raw state in Asturian cooking.
