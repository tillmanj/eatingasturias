{% newthought 'Polenta is a cooked dish' %}, I know that. But as it is sold in Asturias it is very coarsely ground yellow cornmeal. This is important (to me anyway) because there is no American-style medium grind cornmeal to be found in Spain. Likewise there is no white cornmeal at all. So if I want grits, I have to make them from polenta.

## Production
Asturias produces a small amount of polenta-ground cornmeal at the same mills that produce [harina de maíz](/culture/products/harina-de-maíz.html).
