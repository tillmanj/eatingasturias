The Chosco de Tineo festival is held in Tineo conceyo on the first weekend in November every year. Organized by the Ayuntamiento de Tineo in conjunction with the IGP Chosco de Tineo. More information on it can be found on the [council website](https://www.tineo.es/festival-chosco-y-huerta).
